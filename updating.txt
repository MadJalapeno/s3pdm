## Versioning and releases

The version number is `__version__` in `s3vault_core.py` and is shown under **More ▾ → About**.
Every commit record in the vault also stores the app version that wrote it.

To release a new version:

1. Update `__version__` in `s3vault_core.py`.
2. Add an entry at the top of `CHANGELOG.md`.
3. Commit and tag it: `git commit -am "Release 1.1.0"` then `git tag v1.1.0` and `git push --tags`.
